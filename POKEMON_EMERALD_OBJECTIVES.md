# Pokémon Emerald objectives — source of truth

Created: 9 October 2026. Applies to the original Pokémon Emerald, not Ruby/Sapphire, remakes, or ROM hacks.

## How to use this checklist

This file is the authoritative objective and progress tracker for our Emerald playthrough. Consult it before choosing the next gameplay objective and update it after verified milestones. Later explicit user instructions override it.

The default completion target is **become Champion, enter the Hall of Fame, and save**. Optional goals below do not block main-story completion. This is a milestone list; training, healing, supplies, and navigation are supporting actions.

Unchecked means **completion has not been verified**, not necessarily that the objective remains undone. No current save has been inspected for this list. Mark a milestone complete only after observing its outcome in the game or corroborating it with reliably interpreted game state. Record evidence in the progress log; do not infer progress from location alone. Follow prerequisite order, allowing objectives already completed or legitimately completed in another order.

## Required main-story objectives

- [ ] M01 — Finish the Littleroot introduction; choose a starter and rescue Birch.
- [ ] M02 — Beat the rival on Route 103; receive the Pokédex and Poké Balls.
- [ ] M03 — Meet Norman and complete Wally's catching tutorial in Petalburg.
- [ ] M04 — Defeat the Aqua grunt in Petalburg Woods.
- [ ] M05 — Defeat Roxanne in Rustboro: Stone Badge.
- [ ] M06 — Recover the Devon Goods and rescue Peeko in Rusturf Tunnel; receive Devon's delivery assignments and PokéNav.
- [ ] M07 — Deliver Steven's letter in Granite Cave.
- [ ] M08 — Defeat Brawly in Dewford: Knuckle Badge.
- [ ] M09 — Deliver the Devon Goods to Stern at Slateport's Oceanic Museum; defeat the Aqua attackers.
- [ ] M10 — Beat the Route 110 rival and Wally outside Mauville Gym.
- [ ] M11 — Defeat Wattson: Dynamo Badge; obtain Rock Smash.
- [ ] M12 — Clear the Rusturf Tunnel rocks and obtain Strength.
- [ ] M13 — Witness the Meteor Falls incident; defeat Maxie at Mt. Chimney.
- [ ] M14 — Defeat Flannery in Lavaridge: Heat Badge.
- [ ] M15 — Defeat Norman in Petalburg: Balance Badge; obtain Surf.
- [ ] M16 — Cross Route 118; free the Weather Institute from Team Aqua.
- [ ] M17 — Beat the Route 119 rival; obtain Fly.
- [ ] M18 — Obtain Steven's Devon Scope on Route 120; clear Fortree Gym's invisible obstruction.
- [ ] M19 — Defeat Winona: Feather Badge.
- [ ] M20 — Confront Team Aqua at Mt. Pyre; obtain the Magma Emblem.
- [ ] M21 — Enter the Jagged Pass Magma Hideout; witness Groudon awakening and defeat Maxie.
- [ ] M22 — Witness Team Aqua stealing the submarine in Slateport.
- [ ] M23 — Clear Lilycove's Aqua Hideout and open the sea route toward Mossdeep.
- [ ] M24 — Defeat Tate and Liza: Mind Badge.
- [ ] M25 — Defeat Team Magma at Mossdeep Space Center alongside Steven; obtain Dive from Steven.
- [ ] M26 — Reach Seafloor Cavern; defeat Archie and witness Kyogre awakening.
- [ ] M27 — Meet Steven and Wallace in Sootopolis; identify Sky Pillar as the next destination.
- [ ] M28 — Awaken Rayquaza at Sky Pillar; return to Sootopolis and resolve the legendary crisis.
- [ ] M29 — Obtain Waterfall; defeat Juan: Rain Badge.
- [ ] M30 — Reach Ever Grande; pass Victory Road and defeat Wally.
- [ ] M31 — Defeat Sidney, Phoebe, Glacia, and Drake, then Champion Wallace in one successful League challenge.
- [ ] M32 — Verify Hall of Fame entry and the completed game's save.

Ensure the party can use Surf, Strength, Rock Smash, Dive, and Waterfall when the route requires them. Cut and Flash are conveniences rather than universal story gates. Capturing Rayquaza is optional; awakening it is required.

## Optional postgame and completion goals

These are available extensions, not active requirements unless the user selects them.

- [ ] O01 — Receive the National Pokédex and visit the Battle Frontier via the S.S. Tidal.
- [ ] O02 — Earn all seven silver Frontier Symbols.
- [ ] O03 — Earn all seven gold Frontier Symbols.
- [ ] O04 — Defeat Steven in Meteor Falls.
- [ ] O05 — Obtain Beldum from Steven's house.
- [ ] O06 — Catch Rayquaza, Groudon, and Kyogre.
- [ ] O07 — Solve the Sealed Chamber puzzles and catch Regirock, Regice, and Registeel.
- [ ] O08 — Catch the roaming Latios or Latias determined by the postgame TV choice.
- [ ] O09 — Complete all eight Trick House challenges.
- [ ] O10 — Win a Master Rank Contest in each of the five categories.
- [ ] O11 — Complete the Hoenn Pokédex collection; track species requiring trades separately.
- [ ] O12 — Pursue the National Pokédex collection only if compatible external games and trading are available.

Event-ticket destinations and their Pokémon are conditional extras, outside the default scope. Do not assume historical event access is available.
